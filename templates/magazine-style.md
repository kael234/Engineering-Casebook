# Engineering Casebook Magazine Style

## Format
- A4 portrait.
- Three pages are preferred when the issue fits comfortably. Four pages are normal when needed to preserve readability, figure scale and visual rhythm; record a non-empty `page_count_override_reason` in `issue.yml`.
- Never force a three-page result by tightening typography, leading, source text, figure size or module spacing below the established magazine character.
- Page 1: full-page Deep Dive.
- Page 2: Site Problem + Detail/Product case.
- Three-page issue: Page 3 contains both Engineering Wins + Thread + 60-Second Takeaway + archive recall.
- Four-page issue: use Pages 3 and 4 for the two Engineering Wins and closing synthesis. Keep each win together where practical and reserve a comfortable closing band on Page 4 rather than creating a thin overflow page.

## Visual character
Technical magazine, not corporate report. Restrained warm-white page, dark navy/charcoal text, one warm accent colour, generous but efficient spacing, strong serif/sans hierarchy, no decorative gradients or stock-report chrome.

Issue 004 is the visual-rhythm reference: readable body copy, clear separation between modules, diagrams large enough to teach, and visible breathing room around headings, boxes and sources. Do not use the small archival reconstruction PDFs as a visual baseline when an original-quality issue is available.

## Typography
- Normal body target: 9.0-9.5 pt with roughly 1.15-1.25 line-height. Body must never be below 8.5 pt.
- Normal secondary target: 8.5-9 pt. Secondary must never be below 8 pt.
- Normal sources/captions target: 7.5-8 pt. Sources/captions must never be below 7 pt.
- Minimum sizes are emergency floors, not layout targets. If several text classes must sit on their minimums to remain on three pages, use four pages instead.
- Deep-dive title large enough to establish clear hierarchy without consuming technical space.
- Source dossiers are visually legible, not footnotes sacrificed to pagination.

## Spacing and hierarchy
- Preserve clear vertical separation between case headings, body text, figures, decision boxes, Engineer's Notebook boxes and source dossiers.
- Do not stack unrelated modules edge-to-edge merely to consume less height.
- Prefer a little unused space over visibly compressed leading or undersized diagrams.
- Use the extra space on a four-page issue to improve figure scale, line length, captions, source treatment and the closing synthesis rather than simply enlarging decorative elements.

## Page 1 - Deep Dive
Use an asymmetric two-column grid rather than automatic newspaper balancing. Include:
- concise context/metadata strip;
- `YOU ARE THE ENGINEER` decision box where useful;
- chronology or evidence sequence;
- at least one genuine free-body/load-path/engineering-section figure;
- second technical figure for mechanism, failure sequence or intervention;
- meaningful numeric reconstruction where source data permit;
- Engineer's Notebook;
- clearly ranked source dossier.

If the Deep Dive cannot fit comfortably at normal typography, edit repetitive prose before compressing the page. A fourth page is not permission to make Page 1 dense.

## Page 2 - Problems from Practice
Do not force equal 50/50 heights. Allocate space to complexity, typically 55/45 or 50/50. Each case should use the visual form that best teaches it: section, detail, timeline, comparison table, process chain or monitoring plot.

## Engineering Done Well
Two materially different wins. Prefer one structural/civil and one geotechnical/site case. Show what constraint made the solution clever, what alternatives existed, what system/product was actually used, and measured outcome where available.

For a three-page issue both wins share Page 3. For a four-page issue, distribute them across Pages 3 and 4 according to complexity so that neither case, its figures nor its source dossier is cramped. Avoid splitting one case across a page boundary unless there is no cleaner composition.

The closing synthesis band is a real conclusion, not microprint. Give Thread, takeaways and recall enough height to read comfortably.

## Figure vocabulary
Select from:
- context/elevation;
- free-body/load path;
- section/cutaway;
- failure sequence;
- before/after intervention;
- product detail;
- monitoring plot;
- options comparison.

Avoid repeating the same visual composition for every case.

## Source treatment
Every case ends with `Sources for this case`.
- Primary source first and visibly identified.
- Supporting technical sources after it.
- Include organisation, full report/paper title, date/year where available and live hyperlink.
- Prefer 1-3 strong sources over a bibliography-shaped hedge.

## Density
Target 2300-2900 words total, but treat this as an editorial range rather than a quota. Remove low-value repetition before shrinking the design. Preserve normal typography and spacing first; if useful verified content still needs room, use the permitted fourth page.

## PDF quality gate
Render pages to images and inspect visually before publication. Reject clipping, overlaps, unreadable labels, weak hierarchy, excessive dead space, repetitive figure treatment, tiny sources, diagrams that communicate only the headline rather than the mechanics, or a visibly tighter page than the established magazine rhythm solely to remain at three pages.
