# Diagram Standard

SVG is the default technical figure format.

## Minimum figures
- Deep Dive: at least 2 meaningful figures.
- Each other case: at least 1 meaningful figure.

## Useful figure types
Context/elevation; free-body or load path; engineering section; failure sequence; before/after intervention; product detail; monitoring plot; options comparison.

## Rules
- Never entrust technical geometry, dimensions or engineering notation solely to generative imagery.
- Never invent unsupported dimensions.
- Use consistent line weights, arrows, hatching and Casebook palette.
- SVGs must have a `viewBox`, readable labels, title/description metadata, and no external image/font dependencies.
- Every figure records origin and the source IDs from which technical content was derived.
- Sourced plots may be redrawn for clarity but must remain attributed.
- Contextual generated artwork is optional and must not be the sole explanation of a mechanism.
