title: Cellular jet-grout liquefaction mitigation
source_ids: [SRC-0050, SRC-0051, SRC-0052]
case_ids: [CASE-037]
---
Jet-grout columns arranged as cells create a block of improved ground that confines otherwise liquefiable soil and provides a more reliable load-transfer zone for waterfront or retaining structures.
