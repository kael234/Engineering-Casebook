title: External viscous damping for stay-cable vibration control
source_ids: [SRC-0040]
case_ids: [CASE-031]
---
External viscous dampers dissipate stay-cable vibration energy. Full-scale before-and-after testing can verify the achieved damping ratio and distinguish added dissipation from changes in cable frequency.
