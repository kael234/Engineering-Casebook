title: Wetting-induced strength loss in loose end-tipped fill
source_ids: [SRC-0030]
case_ids: [CASE-024]
---
Rainfall or leakage penetrates loose fill, develops a wetted/seepage zone and reduces strength sufficiently for rapid downslope movement or flow-like failure.
