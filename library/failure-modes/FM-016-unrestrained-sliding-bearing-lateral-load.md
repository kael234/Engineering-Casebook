title: Unrestrained sliding bearing under temporary lateral load
source_ids: [SRC-0039]
case_ids: [CASE-030]
---
A low-friction bearing that correctly permits movement in the permanent design can become an instability mechanism during erection when temporary lateral loads are introduced before diaphragms, restraints or other stabilizing elements are active.
