title: Progressive collapse after local bearing-wall loss
source_ids: [SRC-0031, SRC-0032]
case_ids: [CASE-025]
---
Loss of a vertical bearing component can remove support from the structure above; where ties or alternate load paths are inadequate, falling upper structure can then impact and overload lower floors.
