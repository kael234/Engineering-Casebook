title: Progressive slope failure amplified by drainage damage and transient groundwater
source_ids: [SRC-0038]
case_ids: [CASE-029]
---
Initial slope movement can damage surface drainage and change infiltration paths; later rainfall can then raise transient groundwater pressures and accelerate further deformation, creating a progressive feedback loop.
