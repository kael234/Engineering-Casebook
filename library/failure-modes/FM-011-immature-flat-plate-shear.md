title: Immature flat-plate shear failure after premature support removal
source_ids: [SRC-0029]
case_ids: [CASE-023]
---
Removal of formwork or shoring before the in-situ slab has developed adequate shear capacity can shift construction loads into an immature flat plate and trigger shear failure around a column, followed by progressive redistribution.
