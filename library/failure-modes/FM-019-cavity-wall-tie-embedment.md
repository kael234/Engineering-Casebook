---
id: FM-019
kind: failure_mode
title: Inadequate cavity-wall tie embedment under wind suction
source_ids: [SRC-0046, SRC-0047]
case_ids: [CASE-035]
---
Where cavity width, alignment or installation practice leaves too little tie embedded in the outer leaf, wind suction can pull the masonry away from its supports even when the specified tie type is otherwise suitable.
