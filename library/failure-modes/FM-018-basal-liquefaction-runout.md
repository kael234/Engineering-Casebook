title: Basal liquefaction amplifies landslide runout
source_ids: [SRC-0044, SRC-0045]
case_ids: [CASE-034]
---
Rapid loading of loose saturated ground beneath a moving landslide can generate high pore pressures and sharply reduce basal resistance, allowing the failed mass to travel much farther than source-slope geometry alone suggests.
