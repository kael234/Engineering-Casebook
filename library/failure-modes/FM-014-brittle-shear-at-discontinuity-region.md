title: Brittle shear failure at a reinforced-concrete discontinuity region
source_ids: [SRC-0036, SRC-0037]
case_ids: [CASE-028]
---
A thick support region can remain vulnerable when bearing reaction, diagonal compression, tie reinforcement and anchorage do not form a robust internal load path; deterioration can reduce an already brittle margin without producing large warning deformation.
