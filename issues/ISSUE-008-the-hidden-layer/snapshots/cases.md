# Frozen case snapshot for ISSUE-008

## CASE-033 - Fern Hollow Bridge
Sources: SRC-0042, SRC-0043. Evidence grade A. Deep Dive. NTSB determined that collapse initiated at the severely corroded transverse tie plate at the southwest leg-to-shoe transition. The plate was nonredundant, drainage and debris kept the weathering steel wet, and repeated inspection findings did not become completed repairs. Numerical claims are limited to NTSB-reported values.

## CASE-034 - Oso Landslide
Sources: SRC-0044, SRC-0045. Evidence grade A. Site Problem. USGS work distinguishes source-slope failure from exceptional mobility: wet alluvium beneath the moving mass liquefied and reduced basal resistance. Most of the landslide mass itself did not liquefy. The issue does not generalise Oso runout or soil behaviour to other sites.

## CASE-035 - Oxgangs Primary School
Sources: SRC-0046, SRC-0047. Evidence grade A. Detail/Robustness. The independent inquiry concluded that poor-quality construction and inadequate wall-tie embedment were the primary cause of the cavity-wall collapse. The specified minimum embedment was 50 mm. The identified tie type is recorded only because the inquiry documented it; no product defect is alleged.

## CASE-036 - Forth Road Bridge
Sources: SRC-0048, SRC-0049. Evidence grade A. Structural/Civil Engineering Win. Internal cable inspection found corrosion and estimated strength loss. Dehumidification changed the internal cable environment, acoustic monitoring tracked wire breaks, and later inspections were reported by the Scottish Government as finding the system effective in arresting further corrosion.

## CASE-037 - Elliott Bay Seawall
Sources: SRC-0050, SRC-0051, SRC-0052. Evidence grade A. Geotechnical/Site Engineering Win. The project addressed loose liquefiable waterfront fill using a cellular jet-grout ground-improvement block behind the replacement seawall. Construction quantities are limited to values stated in Seattle project documentation; no post-construction earthquake performance is claimed.
