# Frozen case snapshot for ISSUE-007

## CASE-028 - de la Concorde Overpass
Sources: SRC-0036, SRC-0037. Evidence grade A. Deep Dive. A thick reinforced-concrete cantilever support failed after long service. The inquiry and peer-reviewed structural account identify interacting design/detailing, as-built, material, deterioration/water-protection and repair factors. The magazine uses mechanism schematics and does not claim unsupported as-built bar geometry.

## CASE-029 - Ching Cheung Road
Source: SRC-0038. Evidence grade A. Site Problem. GEO concluded the 3 August 1997 landslide was the final stage of progressive failure beginning 7 July, with extremely severe rainfall, adverse groundwater, a high transient perched water table, subsurface natural pipes and drainage disruption contributing to progressive deterioration.

## CASE-030 - Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood Airport
Source: SRC-0039. Evidence grade A. Detail/Product/Temporary Works. OSHA concluded pipe-induced lateral load initiated sliding of precast beams on low-friction PTFE/stainless bearings that lacked incident-stage restraint. Permanent diaphragms were not yet cast and the pipe-support designer lacked bearing information.

## CASE-031 - Penobscot Narrows Bridge
Source: SRC-0040. Evidence grade A. Structural Engineering Win. FHWA full-scale tests measured stay-cable response before and after external viscous dampers. First-mode damping ratios increased from roughly 0.10-0.39 percent to 1.22-2.21 percent, with increases at least fivefold and sometimes fifteenfold.

## CASE-032 - Po Shan Drainage Tunnel
Source: SRC-0041. Evidence grade A. Geotechnical Engineering Win. CEDD documents a groundwater-regulation system of two 3.5 m drainage tunnels, 500 m total length, 172 sub-vertical drains and real-time groundwater monitoring, developed after older horizontal drains showed declining discharge and locally high groundwater during heavy rain.
