### Site Problem — CASE-019 — Oroville Dam: water beneath a concrete slab turned a defect into uplift and erosion
Primary source verified open: Independent Forensic Team, *Oroville Dam Spillway Incident — Final Report*, 5 January 2018, commissioned following FERC direction and hosted/referenced by California DWR/FERC.
