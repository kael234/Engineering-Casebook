# Executive Summary
During Westminster station construction, compensation grouting through Tubes a Manchettes was used to counter differential settlement and tower tilt. Observed response was compared with predictions and used to control later injection locations and volumes. [@SRC-0014]

# Engineer's Notebook
Instrumentation becomes a design tool only when predictions, trigger levels and pre-planned interventions exist before threshold movement occurs.
