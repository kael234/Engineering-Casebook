# Executive Summary
A five-level underground parking garage in Leiden was excavated wet because deep permeable sandy strata made continuous dry excavation difficult. Diaphragm walls, temporary restraint, an underwater concrete bottom and uplift resistance enabled later drawdown and permanent construction. [@SRC-0004]

# Engineering Mechanism
The method controlled hydraulic conditions through construction sequence rather than trying to overpower inflow with blanket pumping. Monitoring of wall movement and surrounding settlement supported back-analysis.

# Engineer's Notebook
Groundwater strategy starts with the objective: dry working space, uplift control, piping control, or protection of neighbours can require different systems.
