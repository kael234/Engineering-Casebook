# Executive Summary
The NTSB attributed the March 2018 FIU pedestrian-bridge collapse to design calculation errors in the member 11/12 nodal region and its deck connection. Large progressive cracking was observed before collapse, yet traffic remained beneath the bridge during remedial work. [@SRC-0005]

# Engineering Mechanism
The case demonstrates the danger of using the same analytical assumptions to design a repair when observed cracking already shows that predicted behaviour is wrong.

# Engineer's Notebook
Unexpected distress is evidence. Before repair, establish why the structure disagrees with the model and reassess construction-stage load paths and critical nodal forces.
