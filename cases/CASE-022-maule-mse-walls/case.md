# Executive Summary
FHWA's reconnaissance after the 27 February 2010 Maule earthquake reported that all mechanically stabilized earth walls observed by the team exceeded expected performance. This stood out against widespread settlement, liquefaction and lateral-spreading effects documented elsewhere along the transportation network. [@SRC-0028]

# Engineering Mechanism
An MSE wall is a composite reinforced-soil mass rather than a thin gravity-retaining face. Closely spaced tensile reinforcement mobilizes the fill as a coherent block while the facing provides local confinement and serviceability. The reconnaissance result is evidence of system robustness, not proof that every MSE wall is earthquake-proof. [@SRC-0028]

# Evidence Gap
The draft does not generalize reinforcement lengths, spacing, facing type or seismic coefficients across the observed walls because the reconnaissance summary does not support a single universal detail.

# Engineer's Notebook
Good geotechnical systems often win by distributing demand through a soil mass instead of concentrating it at one rigid interface. Performance evidence should still be checked against foundation and global stability failure modes.
