# Executive Summary
The NTSB investigation of the 1983 Mianus River Bridge collapse found that a suspended span lost support after lateral displacement of a hanger in a pin-and-hanger assembly. The displacement developed from corrosion-induced forces, while inspection and maintenance deficiencies allowed the deteriorating condition to remain undetected. [@SRC-0026]

# Detail Lesson
A compact connection can govern an entire bridge. The detail must not only carry force when pristine; it must remain inspectable and tolerant of water, debris, corrosion products and movement over decades. Hidden restraint can create force components that were never prominent in the clean drawing. [@SRC-0026]

# Evidence Gap
This draft does not reproduce pin diameters, clearances or corrosion losses without direct extraction from the NTSB record.

# Engineer's Notebook
Inspection access is part of detailing. If the component that can drop the structure cannot be seen, cleaned or measured reliably, the design has inherited a maintenance failure mode.
