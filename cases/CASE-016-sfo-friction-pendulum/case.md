# Executive Summary
The International Terminal design compared conventional moment/braced frames, supplemental damping and seismic isolation against explicit performance objectives. The published project paper describes 267 Friction Pendulum bearings and a design that reduced lobby drift while preserving the architectural concept. [@SRC-0022]

# Engineer's Notebook
Define performance before selecting the structural system. Compare drift, force, vertical-load stability, constructability and architectural consequences rather than optimising only member weight or first cost.
