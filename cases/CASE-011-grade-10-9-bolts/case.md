# Executive Summary
CROSS reported sixteen supposedly Grade 10.9 pre-tensioned crane-base holding bolts all failing within four days by brittle fracture. The reporter suspected delayed hydrogen embrittlement, but the published report did not establish a final metallurgical root cause. [@SRC-0012]

# Engineer's Notebook
A head stamp is evidence, not metallurgy. Safety-critical fasteners need traceable procurement, certificates, process controls and controlled installation. Treat suspected mechanisms as suspected until the investigation establishes them.
