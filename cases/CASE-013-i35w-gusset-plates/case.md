# Executive Summary
NTSB found the I-35W bridge collapse was caused by inadequate U10 gusset-plate capacity from a design error, with increased bridge dead load and concentrated construction loads contributing. Independent analyses supported the connection-level failure mechanism. [@SRC-0016]

# Engineering Mechanism
The connection plate was not a passive detail; it collected large truss forces and required adequate thickness and stability. Concentrated construction staging near U10 mattered differently from the same gross load spread over the planned pour length.

# Engineer's Notebook
Construction-load approval must trace reactions into connectors, gussets, bearings, stiffeners and anchors. A global member check cannot certify an interface omitted from the model.
