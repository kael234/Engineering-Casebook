# Executive Summary
UK HSE guidance uses swimming-pool-building failures, including Uster, to warn that common stainless grades can suffer chloride stress corrosion cracking in warm humid, poorly washed ceiling atmospheres. IMOA identifies Type 304 in the Uster hanger context. [@SRC-0020] [@SRC-0021]

# Engineer's Notebook
Stainless steel is not an exposure class. Record grade, tensile/residual stress, accessibility, cleaning regime and atmosphere for safety-critical hidden hangers and fixings.
