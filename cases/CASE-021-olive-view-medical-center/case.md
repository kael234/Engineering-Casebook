# Executive Summary
The replacement Olive View hospital, designed in 1976 with high lateral strength and stiffness, was strongly shaken during the 1994 Northridge earthquake yet sustained limited structural and nonstructural damage. USGS instrumentation recorded approximately 0.91 g at the free field, 0.82 g at the ground floor and 2.31 g at the roof. [@SRC-0027]

# System
The USGS paper describes concrete shear walls in the lower two stories and perimeter steel shear walls in the upper four stories. The case is valuable because measured response can be compared with observed damage rather than relying on design intent alone. [@SRC-0027]

# Evidence Gap
Acceleration values describe this recorded earthquake response; they are not generic performance limits and must not be presented as such.

# Engineer's Notebook
Instrumentation turns a successful building into evidence. Where consequence is high, measured response can test assumptions that otherwise survive only as models and code checks.
