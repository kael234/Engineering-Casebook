# Executive Summary
At the Adoshi section of the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, high intact basalt strength did not prevent monsoon rockfall because discontinuity orientation, weathered seams and water controlled kinematic release. The case history used stereographic/Markland analysis and documented scaling, mesh/shotcrete and anchored protection measures. [@SRC-0019]

# Engineer's Notebook
Before quoting intact UCS for a rock cut, map joint sets, daylighting planes, wedge intersections, altered seams and crest drainage. Kinematic feasibility and hydraulic conditions can govern before intact strength does.
