# Executive Summary
Twin TBM tunnels existed before a deep top-down station excavation progressively removed soil restraint and overburden. Highly permeable weathered quartz increased seepage risk. The project used targeted relief wells, monitoring and sequencing rather than indiscriminate full dewatering. [@SRC-0011]

# Engineer's Notebook
Define the groundwater objective explicitly: dry excavation, uplift control, basal stability, piping control and protection of neighbouring foundations are different requirements.
