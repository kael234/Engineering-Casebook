# Executive Summary
The stabilisation strategy used temporary counterweighting, detailed monitoring, groundwater control and highly controlled soil extraction beneath the high side of the foundation to reduce inclination. [@SRC-0008]

# Engineering Mechanism
Underexcavation was performed incrementally: observed movement after one extraction informed the next instruction. The monitoring system was therefore part of the design-control loop, not merely post-hoc observation.

# Engineer's Notebook
Observational design requires a prediction, trigger, prepared intervention and short feedback loop. Monitoring without authority to change the work is a weaker risk-control tool.
