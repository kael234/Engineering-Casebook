# Executive Summary
L'Ambiance Plaza collapsed during lift-slab construction in Bridgeport, Connecticut. The NBS/NIST investigation identified the most probable initiating event as loss of support at a lifting jack on the west tower while a package of floor slabs was being positioned. Laboratory work reproduced a mechanism in which excessive deformation of a lifting angle in the shearhead assembly allowed the lifting nut to slip free. [@SRC-0024]

# Engineering Mechanism
During lifting, the temporary jack/shearhead assembly was not a minor accessory: it was the active vertical load path for the slab package. Loss of one support could therefore redistribute demand abruptly into a partially completed structure whose permanent stability system was not yet acting in its final configuration. [@SRC-0024]

# Evidence Gap
This draft does not assign unsupported dimensions, jack loads or reinforcement quantities. The magazine reconstruction must use only geometry and numerical values directly traceable to the investigation.

# Engineer's Notebook
Temporary works become primary structure whenever the permanent load path has not yet been established. Review the lifting state as its own structural system, including single-point support loss and the consequences of local deformation.
