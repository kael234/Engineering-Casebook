# Executive Summary
NIST's 2026 technical findings concluded that two garage-column/pool-deck connections suffered punching-shear failure weeks before the 24 June 2021 collapse. Cracking and load redistribution then overloaded adjacent connections with inadequate reserve.

# Engineering Mechanism
The transferable issue is not simply local punching shear. Once a connection loses stiffness or capacity, load moves into neighbouring slab-column connections. Low original margins, design/construction deviations, later modifications and corrosion reduced the system's tolerance for redistribution. [@SRC-0001]

# Engineer's Notebook
For existing flat slabs, check both local slab-column resistance and the consequence of losing a critical connection: where does the redistributed load go, and is there credible reserve?
