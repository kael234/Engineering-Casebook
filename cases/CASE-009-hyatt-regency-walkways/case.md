# Executive Summary
The as-built two-rod suspension arrangement caused the fourth-floor box-beam connection to transfer both the upper and lower walkway reactions, roughly doubling that connection demand relative to the original continuous-rod concept. NBS found insufficient box-beam/hanger-rod connection capacity; the original detail was itself deficient and the revised arrangement was substantially worse. [@SRC-0009]

# Engineer's Notebook
Any change in support hierarchy, continuity, splice location or hanger arrangement demands a fresh free-body diagram. Ask: what now hangs from what, and which interface receives a new reaction?
