# Executive Summary
The NTSB found that the ceiling collapse in Boston's I-90 Connector Tunnel involved adhesive anchors using Powers Power-Fast Epoxy Injection Gel Fast Set, also supplied as NRC-1000 Gold, with inadequate creep resistance under sustained tensile load. [@SRC-0003]

# Toolbox
The critical product distinction was exact formulation and qualification, not the generic phrase 'epoxy anchor'. Short-term capacity did not demonstrate adequate sustained-load performance.

# Engineer's Notebook
For safety-critical post-installed anchors specify and verify exact product/formulation, substrate, embedment, installation method, orientation, load duration and applicable approval/testing.

# Evidence Gaps
This record does not generalise the incident into a claim that chemical anchors as a class are unsuitable for sustained loading.
