# Executive Summary
A retaining wall designed around drained conditions moved and transferred large lateral actions into the stability system of an adjacent multi-storey steel building. CROSS highlighted drainage detail, discharge capacity and long-term maintainability as key lessons. [@SRC-0002]

# Engineering Mechanism
A design assumption such as 'fully drained backfill' remains valid only while the drainage system continues to perform. Blockage, poor falls and fines migration can introduce hydrostatic pressure and change retained-soil behaviour.

# Intervention
The published case describes improved drainage, added structural restraint and monitoring rather than relying on a single restored drain.

# Engineer's Notebook
Inspect retaining walls as systems: outlets, standing water, settlement, displaced finishes, adjacent frames and anything capable of becoming an unintended load path.
