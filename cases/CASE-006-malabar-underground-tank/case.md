# Executive Summary
An underground reinforced-concrete tank uplifted during monsoon conditions before its full stabilising dead weight existed. The case history reports a construction-stage uplift factor below unity under the observed groundwater condition. Temporary flooding of the tank added ballast; permanent pressure control used peripheral dewatering wells. [@SRC-0006]

# Engineer's Notebook
Buoyancy checks must form a matrix of construction stage versus credible groundwater level. The governing case may occur long before the finished structure exists.
