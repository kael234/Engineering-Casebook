# Executive Summary
Internal inspection of the Forth Road Bridge main cables in 2004-05 found broken and corroded wires and an estimated 8-10% loss of strength. Engineers installed cable dehumidification to reduce moisture inside the wire bundles and acoustic monitoring to detect wire breaks. A 2017 Scottish Government release states that inspections in 2006, 2009, 2012 and 2015 found the dehumidification system effective in arresting further cable corrosion. [@SRC-0048] [@SRC-0049]

# Engineering Mechanism
The intervention changes the corrosion environment inside the cable rather than merely treating the external surface. The monitoring system provides a separate condition signal, while repeated intrusive inspections check whether the preservation claim remains credible.

# Evidence Gap
The sources support effectiveness in arresting further corrosion but do not justify a claim of zero future wire breaks, zero maintenance or unlimited service life.

# Engineer's Notebook
When a preservation system claims to stop a mechanism, monitor the mechanism or a defensible proxy, not merely whether the equipment is running.
