# Executive Summary
Part of an external cavity wall at Oxgangs Primary School collapsed on 29 January 2016. The independent inquiry commissioned by the City of Edinburgh Council concluded that the primary cause was poor-quality construction that failed to achieve the specified minimum 50 mm wall-tie embedment, particularly in the outer leaf. Similar defects were found across the related school estate. [@SRC-0046]

# Engineering Mechanism
Wind suction pulls the outer masonry leaf away from the inner leaf. Wall ties transfer that out-of-plane load through their embedment. Excessive cavity width, non-vertical inner masonry and installation practice can consume the tie length needed for adequate outer-leaf embedment. Once the cavity is closed, the defect becomes difficult to see. [@SRC-0046]

# Evidence Gap
The inquiry identified a Vista VT1 heavy-duty strip tie as the type used in the Oxgangs gable, but did not attribute the collapse to a defective product. This record therefore treats the lesson as installation and quality assurance, not product failure.

# Engineer's Notebook
A minimum embedment note needs a hold point. Check cavity width, tie type, length, spacing and achieved embedment while the work is still visible.
